empirical questions in labor economics and education policy — how people move
between schooling and work, what shapes those transitions, and which policy
levers actually move the outcomes we care about.

Coursework this semester: statistical reasoning in R with David Choi,
intermediate economic analysis with Felix Koenig, and Python. Econometrics and
causal inference next.
