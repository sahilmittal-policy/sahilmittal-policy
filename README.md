**Equitable Bus Priority Corridors, MBTA** · *MIT Policy Hackathon — Second Place* · Nov 2025
Built a Transit Dependence Index across all MBTA bus routes from ACS demographics and 800m stop-level walksheds, ranking every route by equity-weighted ridership to identify the three highest-need corridors. Combined GTFS schedules, MBTA performance feeds and LEHD LODES employment microdata to show that the highest-need corridors also carry the largest service deficits. Modelled a $6.5M bus-priority package returning 1.23M rider-hours annually with a 1.7-year payback.
`GeoPandas` `GTFS` `LEHD LODES` `Cost-benefit analysis`
